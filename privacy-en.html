Pocket Terminal Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

contentsB ("the developer") respects the privacy of everyone who uses the Pocket Terminal app ("the app").

1. Summary

  • The app lets you use the terminal on your own computer (Mac) from your phone. In that terminal you can run AI tools such as Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI, or any other program.
  • Your phone and computer connect through a relay server run by the developer. The connection is end-to-end encrypted between your phone and your computer, so neither the relay nor the developer can see its content, such as terminal output, what you type, or files.
  • The relay only handles the connection data it needs to link the two ends: IP addresses, connection times and data volume (section 4).
  • The app has no ads, analytics or tracking. Personal information is never sold or used for advertising.

2. Information stored on your phone

To keep the connection working, the app stores the following only on your phone (Keychain and app settings). None of it is sent to the developer.

  • Name, room ID, public key and access token of each computer you connect
  • This device's signing key: it is created inside your phone's security chip (iPhone Secure Enclave, Android Keystore) and never leaves it. Only the public key is given to your computer.
  • App settings (such as whether Face ID lock is on)

Deleting the app deletes this information. Removing a computer under "Connected computers" in Settings deletes that computer's connection information.

3. Device permissions

  • Camera: used only to scan the pairing QR code on your computer screen. No photos are saved or sent.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: used only when you give instructions by voice. Your phone's speech recognition turns your voice into text (Apple or Google may process this on their servers under their policies), and the text is sent only to your computer. No recordings are saved.
  • Face ID: your phone checks your face to unlock the app. The app never receives face data.
  • Notifications: used for approval requests from your computer and work updates.

You can turn any permission off at any time in your phone's settings.

4. Relay server and third-party services

  • Pocket Terminal relay server (run by the developer on Cloudflare Workers): links your phone and computer.

- Content: end-to-end encrypted (Noise protocol). The relay only forwards ciphertext, has no keys to decrypt it, and does not store it.

- Data it handles: IP addresses of your phone and computer, connection times, amount of data exchanged, the room ID (derived from your computer's public key), and hashes of approved device public keys.

- Purpose: linking the connection and preventing abuse and excessive use (daily usage limits).

- Retention: only the daily usage counter is kept, for that day, and deleted the next day. The developer keeps no access logs. Records that Cloudflare keeps to operate its service follow Cloudflare's policies.

  • Apple Push Notification service / Google: so you get notifications even when the app is closed, your phone's push token is stored on your computer, and your computer sends notifications through Apple or Google. Notifications carry only short generic text, not content.
  • AI tools (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, etc.): the app only shows the terminal on your computer. Whatever you send to an AI tool is processed on your computer under your own account, and that company's (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, etc.) terms and privacy policy apply.
  • Apple and Google (subscription payments): the app stores handle subscription payments and refunds. The app only checks the store-signed subscription status and never receives your payment details.

5. Processors and international transfer

The developer entrusts the operation of the relay server as follows, and the connection data in section 4 is transferred abroad in the process.

| Recipient | Country | Data | When and how | Purpose | Retention |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Cloudflare, Inc. | United States (global data centers) | IP address, connection time, data volume, room ID | Over the network on each connection | Operating the relay server | As in section 4 |

If you do not want this transfer, please do not use the app (it cannot connect without the relay). The developer does not otherwise provide personal information to third parties.

6. Retention and deletion

Information on your phone is deleted as soon as you delete the app or remove a computer. Information on your computer (approved devices, push tokens) is deleted when you uninstall Pocket Terminal from that computer. Relay data follows section 4.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 14.

8. Your rights

You can delete connection information in the app's Settings or turn permissions off at any time. For privacy questions or requests to access, correct, delete or stop processing information, contact us below.

Users in other jurisdictions, including the European Union and United Kingdom (GDPR) and California (CCPA/CPRA), have the rights their local law provides, such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and the right to complain to a supervisory authority. The developer does not sell personal information or share it for advertising.
